03 / Ask the governed data

Natural language, inspectable answer.

Question to Genie

Inspectable generated SQL · neutral catalog

SELECT region, category,
       100 * SUM(missed_orders)
           / SUM(order_count) AS miss_pct,
       SUM(lost_margin_eur) AS lost_margin_eur
FROM fe_bar_pharmacy_demo.pickup_operations.gold_pickup_daily
WHERE order_date BETWEEN DATE '2026-09-21'
                     AND DATE '2026-09-25'
GROUP BY region, category
ORDER BY miss_pct DESC;

05 / Architect's view

Designed for trust, with visible trade-offs.

What is governed

  • Lakeflow quality expectations passed 120,000 orders with zero rejected rows.
  • Genie queries Unity Catalog on behalf of the signed-in user; generated SQL stays visible.
  • The app identity can read the synced Postgres table, but cannot insert into it.

What a production pilot must address

  • Triggered Lakebase sync is not real time; monitor freshness against the operating SLA.
  • Unity Catalog row/column policies do not automatically become Postgres policies.
  • AI notes are advisory, and supply constraints require human approval.

No patient data, real customer records, or automated clinical decisions are part of this prototype.

06 / Decision and pilot

Prove the intervention, not just the model.

Business hypothesis
30%

Prevented modeled exposure in a comparable five-day event ≈ €28.1k. Validate actual margins and intervention costs before claiming ROI.

Pilot success criteria
  • Run one region and pickup-critical categories with a baseline or holdout.
  • Track missed-pickup rate, recovered margin, action lead time, and false alarms.
  • Reconcile Genie answers to SQL; review access and sync freshness.

Executive ask: sponsor a bounded pilot. Operations ask: define feasible interventions. Technical ask: agree data contracts, identity, and freshness targets.
